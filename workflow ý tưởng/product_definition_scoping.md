# Workflow: Product Definition & Scoping

This workflow focuses on translating a vague product idea into clear success criteria and an AI-leveraged workflow, based on the "Manus prompt guide" and "AI leveraged AIO-product workflow".

## Phase 1: Define Success Criteria for Vague Problems
1. **Unpack the Request:**
   - Ask clarifying questions to the user about the ambiguous parts of the idea.
2. **Establish the "Success Looks Like" Profile:**
   - Define quantifiable or binary outcomes that prove the product works.
   - List non-goals (what the product is NOT supposed to do).

## Phase 2: AI-Leveraged Execution Planning
1. **Map the User Journey:**
   - Detail the steps a user takes through the product.
2. **Identify AI Intervention Points:**
   - Determine where AI (Agents, LLMs) can replace or enhance traditional logic in the user journey.
3. **Create the MVP Scope:**
   - Prune the feature list down to the absolute essentials needed to hit the success criteria.
